# Apple Stage 5 — Product Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The product opens from its card at once — photo, name, price, heart and bar — with the photo growing out of the card; «В корзину» answers on the button («Добавляем…», a check «В корзине» with a light haptic, then «Открыть корзину»); in the cart every change saves itself, like the card's stepper, with a small «Сохранено», and nothing is lost on back; at the smallest step «−» is a bin with «Вернуть»; the note closes its keyboard by «Готово», a tap outside or a scroll, counts from 250 and shows its error as it is typed; a screen reader hears one price phrase, one adjustable quantity and a button that names the product; the quantity sheet opens with the keyboard, quick chips and a live «≈» sum; the estimate is two short lines with «Как считаем»; at 200 % text the sum stands beside the stepper and the button takes the full width.

**Architecture:** The product screen stops calling the cart controllers and goes through `CartQuantityQueue` (`DL-119`), which learns to carry a line's note and rule next to its quantity, so one request per product holds across the card, the cart line and the product screen (the stage 3 residual). The card hands its `CatalogProduct` and a per-card hero tag to the route as `extra`; the screen shows that product until the server's answer replaces it. The page's new pieces go to `product_sections.dart`, the photo's to `product_photo.dart`; `ProductBar` gets a state enum instead of a free label. The quantity sheet is one sheet for its three callers. The keyboard's «Готово» bar is a core piece for later stages.

**Tech Stack:** Flutter 3.47.5, flutter_riverpod 3.4.3, go_router (`extra`), `Hero`, flutter_test (`tester.takeAnnouncements()`, `meetsGuideline(iOSTapTargetGuideline)`, `SystemChannels.platform` mock for haptics, `tester.view.viewInsets` for the keyboard).

**Spec:** `tasks/APPLE_BATCH.md` stage 5 (items `product-1` … `product-9`, `product-4` **repro**, `product-7` **Owner**); the live test «Тест товара - Apple» in the Claude Design project "BarakaBozor — стиль покупателя" (its "стало" state is the picture); `DL-80`, `DL-117`, `DL-119`, `DL-120`, `DL-121`; `frontend/AGENTS.md` §§ 5, 10, 11.
