# Apple Stage 4 — Checkout Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The checkout keeps its layout while the server counts — the goods and the amounts stand in place with grey bars instead of figures; the busy button says what it does; a recount shows "было → стало" right above «Заказать»; «Показать состав» names every product with its server sum; the wish can be typed at once, is checked as it is typed and survives a trip to the cart; without an address the link says «Добавить» and a line says why; payment is one cash row and the line «Онлайн-оплата через Payme и Click — скоро»; large text puts the address link under the address and «Итого» over the button; the placed card rises from the button with a light haptic.

**Architecture:** The screen keeps its preview and placement logic (`DL-50`, `DL-89`) untouched; what changes is what it draws between answers. A `CheckoutItem` list is built from the preview's lines when there is one and from the cart's lines before it (names, quantities, pictures — never amounts), so the goods section never waits. The amounts box stands whenever an address is held, with `SkeletonBar`s that pulse while a preview runs. The wish's draft lives in a per-account `checkoutWishDraftProvider` that outlives the screen and is cleared when an order is placed. The footer and the item strip move into files of their own; the cart footer's "Итого" row becomes a shared `FooterTotal`. `Fold` learns to start hidden and to reverse without a jump (the stage 3 residual).

**Tech Stack:** Flutter 3.47.5, flutter_riverpod 3.4.3, flutter_test (`tester.takeAnnouncements()`, `meetsGuideline(iOSTapTargetGuideline)`, `SystemChannels.platform` mock for haptics).

**Spec:** `tasks/APPLE_BATCH.md` stage 4 (items `checkout-1` … `checkout-10`, `checkout-8` **Owner**); the live test «Тест оформления - Apple» in the Claude Design project "BarakaBozor — стиль покупателя" (its "стало" state is the picture); memory of the review: online payment returns as real options with Wave 5; `DL-50`, `DL-89`, `DL-119`, `DL-120`; `frontend/AGENTS.md` §§ 5, 10, 11.

## Review Focus

1. An address picked while the previous address's preview still runs: no figure of either address may show until the new answer — the amounts and the composition list's sums stay bars, the strip keeps its pictures. Task 5 test `'figures never show for another address, not even in the composition'`.
2. A wish typed while the opening preview runs (the field is now open): «Заказать» must preview the new wish and place that token, never the one made for the old wish. Task 2 test `'a wish typed during the opening preview is previewed again before placing'`.
3. The draft and other accounts or a placed order: a sign-out and another Customer's sign-in start with an empty wish; a placed order empties it; a sent confirmation restored shows the wish it was sent with, locked. Task 2 tests.
4. The recount and what follows it at large text on a 320 px phone: notice, «Итого» row and «Заказать» all fit, «Заказать» stays hit-testable, and the notice goes as soon as the wish or the address changes or the next tap fails. Task 3 tests and the kept test `'after a recount a failing tap shows itself, not the recount'`.
5. An over-long wish when «Заказать» is tapped with the field scrolled off a small screen: the field comes into view with its error and the keyboard focus, nothing is sent. Task 2 test `'Заказать with an over-long wish brings the field into view and focuses it'`.
