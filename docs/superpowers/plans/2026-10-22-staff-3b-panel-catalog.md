# Staff Stage 3b — The Panel's Catalog Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The Admin's products list, product page, picture card and categories look and work like the approved drawings 62–65а of `kit/11-panel-katalog`.

**Architecture:** Client only. The four catalog files under `frontend/lib/features/admin/presentation/` are rebuilt on the panel pieces of stage 3a (`PanelCard`, `PanelMenuButton`, `PanelPager`, `showKitDialog`), with a few more lifted to `core/widgets/panel/` so stage 3c reuses them. The same controllers, providers, server calls, form rules and edit semantics (`DL-28`) stay. Three behaviours are new and need no server change: archiving asks first, a product hidden by its category says so, and a category row opens its window.

**Tech Stack:** Flutter 3.47.5, Riverpod 3, go_router, `flutter gen-l10n` (template `app_uz.arb`), Montserrat, `KitColors`.

**Spec:** the Claude Design project "BarakaBozor — стиль покупателя", file `kit/11-panel-katalog.dc.html` (62 товары, 62а–62в, 63 страница товара, 63а–63и, 64 категории, 64а–64в, 65–65а планшет), approved by the Owner ("да", 2026-10-22); styles `kit/panel.css`, `kit/panel-katalog.css`. The Owner's rule is `DL-97`; the agent's rules are `DL-101`, one item per task. The catalog's existing rules are `DL-28`; the panel's frame and pieces are `DL-100` and `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-22-staff-3a-panel-orders.md`.

## Review Focus

1. **The width boundaries.** A products card of 799/800, a page content of 999/1000, a categories card of 559/560 switch their layouts; at 820×1180 ×2 and 1440×900 ×2, in `uz` and `ru`, nothing overflows and every action stays reachable. Tests in Tasks 2, 4 and 5 sit on both sides of each boundary.
2. **An archive that runs or fails.** While an archive runs, its window holds through the ×, the scrim and Escape; a refusal (`409 business_conflict`) shows inside it and nothing is archived on "Отмена"; a restore stays one tap with no window. Task 2 tests "a product is archived only after the window's confirm" and "an archive in flight holds its window and a refusal shows in it", Task 5 test "archive hides a category until archived ones are shown, then it is restored".
3. **What hides a product.** The category line and the page note show only for a product that is itself shown (`state == active`) in a hidden or archived category, with the matching words; a hidden or archived product, or one whose category is not among the loaded options, shows none and does not fail. Task 2 test "the list says which products their category hides", Task 4 test "the page says when the chosen category hides the product".
4. **The customer price while the price is being edited.** The box shows the saved customer price and the saved market price it comes from, never a figure computed on the client; a new product shows "Появится после сохранения". Task 4 test "the customer price box names the saved price it comes from".
5. **Long names at ×2.** A 160-character product name and a 120-character category name in the table, on a card, in the page title and in the windows wrap or cut with an ellipsis and never overflow. Tests "the products list holds at the extremes (uz|ru)" (Task 2), "the product page holds at the extremes (uz|ru)" (Task 4), "the categories hold at the extremes (uz|ru)" (Task 5).
