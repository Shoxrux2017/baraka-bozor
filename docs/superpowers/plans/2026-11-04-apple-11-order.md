# Apple Stage 11 — «Заказ» Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The Customer's order page keeps the order when a refresh fails («Нет связи · данные на 12:46 · Обновить», pull to refresh). A Shopper's question shows what each answer does to the bill («Согласитесь: +5 300 сум», «Откажетесь: товар уберут, −20 000 сум»), the replacement's photo, and «Осталось 9 мин» that turns red at 3 and expires the card at once. An answered card folds into «Вы согласились: 16 500 сум за кг» on its line, with a haptic and an announcement; a new question unfolds, or, above what the Customer is reading, arrives without moving the page and with a «Сборщик спрашивает ↑» pill; its line flashes. The stages read at 12 px with a visible track, checks and «Этап 2 из 4»; at large text they stand as a list and line sums go under the names. Removed lines stand under «Убрали из заказа · 2» without «0 сум», and the header and the amounts count the same lines.

**Architecture:** The server makes the open line's estimate follow what the Customer agreed to (price in force × quantity in force), says so on the line (`agreed`), and gives each open question the change each answer makes to the line and the replacement's picture. On the client the question card reads those fields; `OrderScreen` keeps the last order under `StaleDataLine`; the lines are two `FoldingColumn`s (kept and removed); the question blocks are `Fold`s whose arrival is measured folded first, then either unfolded in view or shown still with a scroll correction.

**Tech Stack:** Laravel 12 + PostgreSQL, Flutter 3.47.5, flutter_riverpod 3.4.3, go_router 18.1.2, core `Fold`/`FoldingColumn`, `StaleDataLine`, `DeadlineChip`/`minutesLeft`, `announce`, `textScaleAt`/`textWidth`, `HapticFeedback`, `ScrollPosition.correctBy`, flutter_test (`tester.takeAnnouncements()`, `meetsGuideline(iOSTapTargetGuideline)`, `NonlinearTextScaler`, `SystemChannels.platform` mock for haptics).

**Spec:** `tasks/APPLE_BATCH.md` stage 11 (`order-2` **repro**, `order-3` **Owner** **server**, `order-4` **server**, `order-5` … `order-10`); the live test «Тест заказа - Apple» in Claude Design (no local copy yet — see «Before the tasks»; values marked ⟨page⟩ below are the plan's own until it is read); the stage track drawing of «Тест списка заказов - Apple» (local copy `G:/tools/tmp/w-ord/site/ord.html`, rules `.bs`, `.bl`, `.vr`, `stageVals`); memory note of the order review (2026-10-24/25); `DL-37` (10), `DL-54` (5), `DL-59` (3), `DL-71`, `DL-119`, `DL-121`, `DL-125`, `DL-126`; `docs/09-api-contracts.md` §20, §23; `frontend/AGENTS.md`, `backend/AGENTS.md`.
