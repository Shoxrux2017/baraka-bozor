# Wave 2 — Cart and order on cash

Status: **In progress** (planned 2026-09-27). Plan under workflow v6 (`tasks/README.md`). Scope from `DL-5` and `docs/06-roadmap.md` sections 2 and 3; engineering decisions for the wave in `DL-37`.

## Goal

The Customer can order: fill a cart from the catalog, check out to a saved address with cash (online shown as unavailable), see the order with its total — an estimate, or guaranteed prices when every line is fixed — and edit it or cancel it until shopping starts. The Admin and the Operator see every order on a board in the web panel — filters, the summary strip, the attention list's first entries — and assign a Shopper, or reassign one, before shopping starts. A Shopper or Courier reaches their own cart through Customer mode. Shopping itself, the Shopper's screens and the Courier's assignment are Wave 3.

## Owner gate

None (`docs/06` section 5). The MapKit key the Owner set aside (`DL-36`) blocks nothing here: a build without it takes the address point as coordinates.

## Task notes

**W2-1.** Forward migrations exactly as `docs/08` sections 9, 10, 13 to 16 and 27 describe them, with the Wave 1 conventions (`DL-18`): UUID keys, `timestamptz`, named checks, `RESTRICT` foreign keys, partial unique indexes (`carts (customer_id) WHERE status='active'`, `order_shopper_assignments (order_id) WHERE ended_at IS NULL`). `orders_order_number_seq` starts at 1001 (`DL-37` (2)). `order_items` carries every column of section 14 now, `markup_percent_snapshot` among them (`DL-37` (8)), although Wave 3 fills most of them, so no later migration reshapes a table with rows in it; the same for `orders`, and `order_history` gains `details jsonb` (`DL-37` (9)). The `event_type` and `reason_code` checks take the vocabularies of `docs/08` section 15 and `DL-3` S-9. `order_courier_assignments`, `customer_approvals`, `order_cancellation_requests`, `order_item_price_corrections`, `payments` and below are created by the wave that first needs them. Models with casts, `$fillable` kept to what input may set (`DL-18` (2)), factories that can build an order in any of the nine states for later tests. Tests: every check, unique and partial index; the sequence; a rollback.

**W2-2.** `App\Support\Idempotency`, beside `Money` and `Scope`, because shopping, delivery and payment actions of later waves use it too. `begin(actor, operation, key, requestHash)` → `new | replay(resource) | in_progress | conflict`, `complete(resourceType, resourceId)`, per `docs/07` section 17 and `DL-3` S-11, with the transaction boundary and the hash of `DL-37` (5): `begin` commits the `processing` row on its own; the operation's writes and `complete` commit together; a business refusal deletes the row, so a retry is judged again rather than told `in_progress`. `409 idempotency_in_progress` inside the 60-second lease, a takeover after it, `409 idempotency_key_reused` for another hash, `400 idempotency_key_required` without the header, and a key that is not a UUID `422 validation_failed`; `complete` and the refusal's delete are conditional on the attempt's own `attempt_token` (`DL-37` (5)). Tests: an attempt that outran its lease neither completes over nor deletes the takeover's record; each outcome; the lease boundary under a controlled clock; two concurrent requests with one key, against a second connection with real commits (`DL-39` (6)); the same key on two different orders answered `idempotency_key_reused`; a refusal followed by a successful retry with the same key.

**W2-3.** `App\Modules\Orders` (`DL-37` (1)). `GET /customer/cart` creates the active cart on first access (`BR-CART-001`); `POST /customer/cart/items`, `PATCH|DELETE /customer/cart/items/{item}` per `docs/09` section 17. Every mutation locks the active cart row first (`DL-37` (7)). `QuantityPolicy` per `BR-QTY-001` with the bounds of `DL-37` (6). A product must be active in an active category to be added (`BR-CAT-003`); a hidden product and an id that does not exist answer the same `409 product_unavailable` (`DL-37` (20), keeping `DL-22` (2)); a line whose product became unavailable stays in the cart with `is_available: false` and outside the subtotal, so the Customer sees why checkout refuses it. The response carries current customer prices, each line's estimate total and `estimated_subtotal_uzs` from the current markup (`BR-CART-003`). Duplicate product → `409 cart_item_already_exists`. Tests: unit precision per unit, the bounds, the 101st line, another Customer's item a scope-safe `404`, archived products, prices following the markup, a change waiting on the cart lock another connection holds (`DL-40` (5)).

**W2-4.** `WorkingHours` in `Asia/Tashkent` (a pair where closing is before opening spans midnight; `00:00`–`23:59` is always open, `DL-19` (8)); `ServiceFeeCalculator` per `BR-MONEY-005`. `POST /customer/checkout/preview` per `docs/09` section 18 with every check of `BR-CHK-001` to `BR-CHK-009`, the settings' completeness first because the service-area and minimum checks need it (`DL-37` (21)); `online` answers `409 payment_method_unavailable` in this wave whatever the enablement switches say (`DL-37` (3)). The signed token of `DL-37` (4). A foreign or deactivated address is the scope-safe `404`. The lines come from `CartView` (`DL-40` (6)); each line's stored quantity is checked again against its product's current unit and bounds, since an Admin may change a product's unit under a line (`1.500` kg becoming 1.5 pieces), and such a line is refused like an unavailable one. Tests: each refusal with its `details`; `final` versus `estimate`; the working-hours note across midnight, at the boundaries and for the always-open pair; the token refused after five minutes and after any bound change (cart, line, unit, product price, markup, fee, address point, payment method, delivery wish), and when forged or signed under another key.

**W2-5.** `POST /customer/orders` (`Idempotency-Key`, `{"checkout_token"}`; `RequireIdempotencyKey` after the authentication and role middleware, so a guest is `401` before a missing key is `400`; the replay loads the order through the Customer's own scope, `DL-39`): lock the cart, read its lines, their products and the settings once, recompute the digest from those values and compare (`409 checkout_snapshot_stale` on any difference or expiry), re-run the preview's checks, then create in the same transaction the order with every snapshot of `docs/07` section 13 taken from the very values the digest covered, its items with their markup snapshot, a `status_changed` history row (`null → new`), the cart `converted`, a new empty active cart (`BR-CHK-008`), and the next order number — read back with a `refresh()`, since the sequence default is not returned by the insert; answer `201` with the order resource of `docs/09` section 20. `GET /customer/orders` (own, newest first, paginated) and `GET /customer/orders/{order}` with the totals of `DL-37` (10) and `can_edit`, `can_cancel_directly`, `can_request_cancellation` computed from the state. No push yet (`DL-37` (15)). Tests: a replay returns the same order and creates nothing; the stale paths; snapshots unchanged after a catalog, markup and settings change; two concurrent creations from one cart make one order; a cart write racing the creation either lands before the lock (and makes the token stale) or after it — an add in the new cart, a change or removal of an old line the scope-safe `404`; a cancelled order's totals `null` with the kind `none`.
